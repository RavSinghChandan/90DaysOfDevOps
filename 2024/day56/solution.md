
# Day 56: Understanding Ad-hoc Commands in Ansible 🚀

Ansible ad hoc commands are like your compact, one-liner Linux shell commands—quick, effective, and perfect for specific tasks across multiple machines. They’re your Swiss Army knife in the world of automation!

🔧 **What are Ansible Ad-hoc Commands?**:
- Quick and specific tasks to execute across servers.
- Not playbooks—these are like a collective of many commands with logic.

### **Task-01**:
- ✨ **Ping**: Write an Ansible ad hoc `ping` command to check connectivity to 3 servers from your inventory file.
- ⏳ **Uptime Check**: Write an Ansible ad hoc command to check the uptime of those servers.

🌐 **Additional Learning**: To deepen your understanding, check out this [blog](https://www.middlewareinventory.com/blog/ansible-ad-hoc-commands/) that provides different examples of ad hoc commands. Try them out, post screenshots of the output in your blog, and share your learnings!
